## Step 2: Submit to Search Engines (15 minutes)

### Google Search Console
1. Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Click "Add Property"
3. Enter: `https://irfanshaikhportfolio.netlify.app`
4. Choose "HTML file" verification
5. You already have the file! (`googled30489a4b749557c.html`)
6. Click "Verify"
7. Go to "Sitemaps" in left menu
8. Submit: `https://irfanshaikhportfolio.netlify.app/sitemap.xml`
9. Click "Submit"

✅ **Result**: Google will crawl your site within 24-48 hours

### Bing Webmaster Tools
1. Go to: https://www.bing.com/webmasters
2. Sign in with Microsoft/Google account
3. Click "Add a site"
4. Enter: `https://irfanshaikhportfolio.netlify.app`
5. Choose "BingSiteAuth.xml" method
6. Copy your verification code (looks like: `12A3B4C5D6E7F8G9H0I1J2K3L4M5N6`)
7. Open `public/BingSiteAuth.xml` in your code
8. Replace `YOUR_BING_VERIFICATION_CODE` with your actual code
9. Redeploy (git commit + push)
10. Go back to Bing and click "Verify"
11. Submit sitemap: `https://irfanshaikhportfolio.netlify.app/sitemap.xml`

✅ **Result**: Will appear on Bing, Yahoo, DuckDuckGo in 3-7 days
