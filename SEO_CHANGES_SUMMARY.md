## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Not indexed on Google after 1 week?
1. Check Google Search Console for errors
2. Manually request indexing
3. Ensure robots.txt allows crawling
4. Verify sitemap is accessible

### Not showing on Bing after 2 weeks?
1. Verify BingSiteAuth.xml is correct
2. Check Bing Webmaster Tools for errors
3. Manually request crawling
4. Ensure site is accessible

### Ranking not improving?
1. Need more backlinks (most common issue)
2. Competitors may have better content
3. Domain authority builds slowly
4. Keep creating content consistently
